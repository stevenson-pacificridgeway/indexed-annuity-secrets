The Idea

Grow safely — without the market risk

As you near retirement, the goal shifts from "how do I get the highest return?" to "how do I make sure I don't lose what I've built?" A fixed indexed annuity is designed to do both: capture market-linked growth while protecting your principal from market losses.

1. Protect

A 0% floor means that in a year the market falls, you earn zero — you never lose principal to the downturn.

2. Grow

In a year the market rises, you earn market-linked interest, up to a set cap — real growth, locked in and protected.

3. Income

When you're ready, turn your savings into predictable income — in many cases, guaranteed for life.
